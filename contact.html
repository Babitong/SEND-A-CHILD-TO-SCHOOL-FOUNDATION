Frequently Asked Questions

How can we help?

How can I donate?

You can visit our Donate page to view the available donation methods and payment information.

How can I volunteer?

Visit our Get Involved page to learn about possible volunteer opportunities and areas where people can contribute their skills and time.

How can an organization partner with the foundation?

Organizations, businesses, schools, community groups and other potential partners can contact the foundation to discuss areas of collaboration and shared goals.

How can I contact the foundation?

You can use the contact form on this page. Once the foundation's official contact information is added, visitors will also be able to reach the organization directly by email, phone or WhatsApp.

Can I report a safeguarding concern?

Yes. However, sensitive child-protection information should not be submitted through a general contact form. The foundation should provide a designated safeguarding contact for such concerns.
