Responsible Storytelling

Tell stories with dignity

Children should never be reduced to statistics or used simply to create emotional reactions. Their dignity, privacy, safety, and best interests must remain central to every story we publish.

Before publication, stories and photographs should be reviewed according to the foundation's safeguarding, consent, and privacy requirements.

✓

Protect Identity

Do not publish sensitive identifying information without appropriate authorization.

✓

Seek Appropriate Consent

Obtain the required consent before using photographs, interviews, or personal stories.

✓

Preserve Dignity

Avoid portraying children or families in ways that exploit vulnerability.

✓

Tell the Full Story

Show people as individuals with aspirations, strengths, and agency—not only as beneficiaries.
