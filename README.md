# SEND-A-CHILD-TO-SCHOOL-FOUNDATION
Send a Child to School Foundation works to remove barriers that prevent vulnerable children and young people from accessing and remaining in education.



> **Every Child in School. Every Dream Within Reach.**

**Motto:** Education Today, Hope for Tomorrow.

## 📖 About the Project

This repository contains the official website for the **Send a Child to School Foundation (SCS Foundation)**, a nonprofit foundation focused on helping vulnerable children access and remain in education.

The foundation works to remove barriers to education through initiatives such as educational assistance, mentorship, life-skills development, health and well-being support, community partnerships, advocacy, and skills development.

The website is designed to serve as the foundation's:

- Official online presence
- Information hub
- Donation platform
- Volunteer recruitment platform
- Partnership platform
- Advocacy platform
- Storytelling platform
- Contact point

---

## 🎯 Foundation Vision

**“Empowering every child through education, hope, and opportunity.”**

## 🎯 Foundation Mission

The foundation seeks to remove barriers to education by providing:

- Scholarships
- School supplies
- Mentorship
- Life-skills training
- Healthcare support
- Community partnerships

## 🌍 Overall Goal

> **No child should be denied quality education because of poverty.**

The foundation also aims to nurture future leaders who positively transform their communities.
