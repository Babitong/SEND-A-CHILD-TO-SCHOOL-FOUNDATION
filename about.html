About Us

Education Today.
Hope for Tomorrow.

We believe every vulnerable child deserves the opportunity to access quality education, develop their potential, and build a better future.

Who We Are

Helping children turn opportunity into possibility.

Send a Child to School Foundation works to remove barriers that prevent vulnerable children and young people from accessing and remaining in education.

Our approach goes beyond school fees. We support education, personal development, practical skills, mentorship, health and well-being, and opportunities that can help young people become empowered members of their communities.

We believe education can open the door to hope, opportunity, leadership and positive transformation.

Our Vision

Empowering every child through education, hope, and opportunity.

We envision a generation of educated, empowered, value-driven leaders where vulnerable children have access to quality education, holistic development, and equal opportunities.

Our Mission

Removing barriers to education.

We seek to remove barriers to education by providing scholarships, school supplies, mentorship, life-skills training, healthcare support, and community partnerships that empower vulnerable children and young people.

Access

Helping children overcome financial and practical barriers to education.

Development

Supporting mentorship, life skills, leadership, digital and vocational development.

Opportunity

Building partnerships and pathways that help young people pursue their potential.
