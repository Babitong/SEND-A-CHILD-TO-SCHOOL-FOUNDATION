Our Programs

Turning support into opportunity.

Our work focuses on helping vulnerable children access education, remain in school, develop their potential, and prepare for the future.

What We Do

Supporting the whole journey of a child.

Our programs are designed to address barriers to education while supporting children's development, well-being, skills, leadership, and future opportunities.

🎓

Program 01

Educational Assistance

Providing educational assistance to vulnerable children and young people who face financial barriers to schooling.

  • ✓ School fees assistance
  • ✓ School materials
  • ✓ Uniforms
  • ✓ Educational support for vulnerable children
⚖️

Program 02

Equal Access to Education

Promoting equal access to education for children regardless of gender, disability, religion, ethnicity, or economic background.

Inclusion

Supporting children who may face barriers because of disability or other circumstances.

Equity

Working toward opportunities that are accessible to children from different backgrounds.

🏫

Program 03

Keeping Children in School

Working to reduce school dropout through continuous support, follow-up, and attention to the challenges that can interrupt a child's education.

Access is only the beginning.

Our objective is to help children remain engaged in education and continue their journey toward graduation.

🌱

Program 04

Youth Leadership & Mentorship

Identifying, mentoring, and developing young people with the goal of nurturing future leaders who can positively contribute to their communities.

  • ✓ Mentorship
  • ✓ Leadership development
  • ✓ Life skills
  • ✓ Values development
💻

Program 05

Skills, Career & Entrepreneurship

Preparing young people for future opportunities through career guidance, entrepreneurship, digital literacy, and vocational skills.

Career Guidance

Helping young people understand education and career pathways.

Entrepreneurship

Developing entrepreneurial knowledge and opportunity-focused thinking.

Digital Literacy

Building practical digital skills for an increasingly connected world.

Vocational Skills

Supporting practical skills development and pathways to opportunity.

❤️

Program 06

Health & Well-being

Recognizing that children need more than classroom access to thrive, the foundation's objectives include health, nutrition, and psychosocial support.
